# ERP Quick Login: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 October 2026

ERP Quick Login is an unofficial browser extension that fills the login form of the IIT Kharagpur ERP (erp.iitkgp.ac.in). It is not affiliated with or endorsed by IIT Kharagpur.

## What the extension stores

When you choose to save them in the extension's settings, the extension stores:

- your ERP User ID (roll number or employee code)
- your ERP password
- your security questions and their answers
- the text of security questions the ERP login page has shown you
- your settings (for example, whether to press "Get OTP" automatically)

## Where it is stored

Everything is stored with the browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your own device. It is not synced to your Google or Microsoft account, not uploaded to any server, and the developer never receives it.

## How it is used

The data is used for one purpose only: when you open the ERP login page, the extension types your saved details into the login form and presses "Get OTP". The ERP page then sends them to erp.iitkgp.ac.in, exactly as if you had typed them yourself.

The extension does not send data anywhere else. It has no servers, analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party code.

## Sharing

Your data is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone. It is not used for advertising, for creditworthiness or lending decisions, or for any purpose unrelated to filling the ERP login form.

## Permissions

- **storage**: to save your details on your device.
- **Access to erp.iitkgp.ac.in**: to fill the login form. The extension cannot read or change any other website.

## Security

Your details are stored in your browser profile without extra encryption. Anyone who can use your computer account and browser could read them. Don't use this extension on a shared or public computer.

## Deleting your data

To delete your data, open the extension, clear the fields and click Save, or remove the extension from your browser. Removing the extension deletes everything it stored.
